PROFILE

About Marika

Marika Kurata is a third-year student with a sharp tongue, a charismatic presence and a talent for turning even the most ordinary situation into something entertaining. She is witty, confident and rarely afraid to say exactly what is on her mind.

As a member of the photography club, Marika has an eye for interesting moments and a natural instinct for presentation. She enjoys capturing people at their most expressive and has a particular appreciation for beautiful, dramatic or simply unexpected scenes.

Marika can come across as carefree and hedonistic, but there is considerably more calculation behind her actions than she lets people see.

She knows how to charm others, how to manipulate a conversation and, most importantly, how to keep everyone wondering what she will do next.
