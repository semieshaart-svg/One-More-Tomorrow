A school made for creators.

What makes Seiran different?

At Seiran, creativity isn't just an extracurricular activity.
It's a way of life.

The academy encourages students to explore their talents and develop their own unique style.

Art, fashion, design, performance and other forms of self-expression are deeply connected to everyday student life.

The result is a school filled with colorful personalities, ambitious students and plenty of unexpected stories.
