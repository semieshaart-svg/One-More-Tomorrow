About Urara

Urara Tsurumaki is a funny and energetic first-year student who belongs to the Music Club. She is outgoing, affectionate and always ready to bring some energy into the lives of the people around her.

She has a strong interest in fashion and perfume, and her hobby is modeling. Urara enjoys expressing herself through her appearance and loves anything that lets her show her stylish side.

Despite her energetic personality, Urara can be impulsive, irresponsible and reckless. She often acts before thinking, which can occasionally get her into trouble.

Urara is also adventurous and energetic, with a humorous personality and a naturally affectionate attitude toward her friends.
