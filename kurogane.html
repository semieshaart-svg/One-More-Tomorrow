A school made for innovators.

What makes Kurogane different?

At Kurogane, curiosity is more than a personality trait.
It's the beginning of every idea.

Students are encouraged to explore science, engineering, technology and other technical disciplines.

Lessons often continue beyond the classroom, with students developing projects, prototypes and experiments.

The academy attracts ambitious students who aren't afraid to ask difficult questions or challenge established ideas.
