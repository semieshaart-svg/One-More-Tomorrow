01 / ABOUT

A school built for excellence.

What makes Silvercrest different?

At Silvercrest, success is not something students simply hope for.
It is something they work toward.

Students are surrounded by rigorous academics, prestigious programs and opportunities designed to prepare them for the future.

The academy values discipline, confidence and leadership, while encouraging students to discover what kind of person they want to become.

Competition can be intense, but friendships and personal connections can become just as important as achievements.
