01 / ABOUT

A school made for dreamers.

What makes Yumehara different?

At Yumehara, imagination isn't something students are expected to leave at the classroom door.
It's where everything begins.

Students are encouraged to explore visual arts, music, performance, literature and other creative fields.

The academy gives students the freedom to experiment with unusual ideas, discover their own artistic voice and create without fear of failure.

Some students dream of becoming professional artists. Others simply want a place where they can be completely themselves.
