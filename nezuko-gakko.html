About Nezuko

Nezuko is a quiet and rather timid student who prefers books, quiet corners and familiar routines. She often seems distant at first, but in reality she simply needs some time to become comfortable around people.

She is especially fond of literature and calligraphy, spending most of her free time in the library. Nezuko tends to notice small details that others overlook and remembers even the tiniest acts of kindness shown to her.

Although she rarely seeks attention, she secretly wishes she could become more confident and courageous. Her current goal is to slowly step outside her comfort zone — one small thing at a time.

Chapter I — Childhood

Before she became brave.

Nezuko was always a quiet child. While other children were busy making noise and chasing one another, she was usually somewhere with a book in her hands.

She discovered calligraphy at a young age and quickly became fascinated by the way simple strokes could turn into something beautiful. It became one of the few things she could do without worrying about being judged.

Even then, she was sensitive to the feelings of people around her. She often noticed when someone was sad or uncomfortable, even when they tried to hide it.
