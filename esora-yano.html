PROFILE

About Esora

Esora Yano is a responsible second-year student who takes her position in the student council seriously. She is organized, composed and always willing to take on another responsibility, even when her schedule is already overflowing.

She has a particular interest in history and tends to approach most things with a thoughtful and analytical mindset. Esora often prefers to observe a situation carefully before deciding what should be done.

Although she may appear strict or intimidating at first, Esora cares deeply about the people around her. She simply has a tendency to express that concern through responsibility, advice and quietly making sure everything is under control.

She also secretly enjoys being praised for her work, even though she rarely admits it.

Her Story

CHAPTER I · CHILDHOOD

Before she became responsible.
