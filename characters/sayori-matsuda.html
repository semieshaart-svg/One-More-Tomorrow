PROFILE

About Sayori

Sayori is a bright, energetic and affectionate girl who has a natural talent for making people feel welcome. She is almost always smiling and tries to keep the atmosphere around her cheerful, even when things aren't going perfectly.

She loves spending time with her friends, baking homemade sweets and coming up with little ways to make ordinary days more special. Sayori can be surprisingly thoughtful beneath her playful personality, often noticing when someone is quiet or upset before anyone else does.

Although she appears carefree, Sayori sometimes struggles with feelings she doesn't know how to express. She tends to hide her worries behind jokes and smiles, convinced that keeping everyone else happy is more important than talking about herself.
